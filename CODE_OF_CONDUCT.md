# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must participate professionally, respectfully, and
without harassment. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks,
discrimination, intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, threats, and the
publication of another person's private information are not.

Examples of positive behavior include focusing criticism on claims and code,
acknowledging uncertainty, documenting evidence, accepting correction, and
protecting reporters who disclose security issues responsibly.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
artifacts, project communication, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, or
other participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid publishing sensitive report
details.

Report conduct concerns privately to `roque@priveperfumeshn.com`. For security
vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant while remaining
specific to this project's current maintainer structure.
