# RGA calibration-layer premise round — synthesis

Working tree, 2026-08-21, before any calibration kernel existed. Four adversaries — verifiability, ceremony-collapse, cheaper formulation (with literature search), incentive dynamics — attacked the proposed escape-ledger design (C1–C7); 28 non-observation findings each went to a skeptic. Verdict: **the premise survives, heavily narrowed, with one construction that changes the design and one amputation.**

## The construction that changes everything

The verifiability attacker's kills-premise finding — checker-claim fidelity is unfalsifiable, so an escape is "a report wearing a seal's clothes" — was **refuted by demonstration on the unmodified kernel**: an escape does not need a new checker. It is a **counterfactual trial of the claim's pinned refuter at a finder-chosen nonce**. The finder authors nothing but a nonce and hands over the bytes; the kernel checks seal membership, hashes the bytes itself against `seal.artifact_hash`, derives the seed with its own `derive_seed` over the sealed hash and the pinned `(id, version)` read from the journal, and requires verdict `refuted` plus one identical replay. Four conjuncts, all shapes the kernel already has (V8, V9, R5, R6). Checker-claim fidelity of this channel is exactly the fidelity the original seal already assumed — no new assumption of any species. The demonstrated bypass checkers (always-refute, hardcoded-witness) are all rejected: their code never runs.

Consequence — **two escape tiers**:
- **Tier A (automatic):** pinned-refuter counterfactual trials. Drive impeachment and charges with no adjudication. Where the pinned refuter ignores its seed (exhaustive/fixed-input), this channel is empty — correct semantics, not a gap.
- **Tier B (adjudicated):** any newly declared checker's kill acts only through a journaled operator decision event — which *is* the per-escape B8 adjudication, named and visible. Nothing automatic.

## The amputation

Automatic demotion on an absolute charge count was found negative-value as drafted (instrument assassination by true filings; audit disincentive; predictable e_max inflation). What survives: **demotion is a pure query** (charges > declared per-class budget), never an event cascade; it blocks new pins at Open; whether it also gates Seal is a **declared per-class policy bit** — and the fail-closed default argument (two verifiers: a crossing is information about the line's only evidence; the machine already closes on declared-floor crossings) won over the griefing argument once charges were made inflation-proof.

## Settled by the round

1. **Charge unit is kernel-fixed**: write-once per `(line, claim, refuter version)` — the journaled wrong-verdict cell. Fifty witnesses of one miss are one charge (the only finding to survive fully intact). Budd–Angluin undecidability applies to defects, not to cells.
2. **Filing is single-holder**: the kernel hashes filed bytes (the `sample()` pattern); naming a hash is assertion.
3. **Escape validity is read at query time** as established ∧ checker-not-refused — the `tainted()` pattern.
4. **Ratchet enforced at Install**, not Seal: every referenced D must be Measured (id-set kernel-fixed) before Install — always achievable since `measure()` reads no policy state — and must cover the class corpus minus journaled exclusions. `install()` must finally **emit a journal event** (today it emits nothing), carrying coverage and exclusion primaries and the predecessor-diff. Bounded-only claims in a class with a nonempty corpus are refused at Install absent an exclusion decision.
5. **Exclusions are priced by visibility, not budget**: per-item enumerated, attributed, with primaries (excluded count, corpus size, position) carried on the install record and every seal under it. Bulk-exclusion "ceremony" was refuted: exclusion waives only successor-coverage; charges, impeachment, track records and the append-only corpus stay monotone and untouched.
6. **Track record**: primaries only, stamped at seal position — charged cells, seals participated, corpus provenance split, exclusion counts — with the stated reading that zero charges is absence of filed evidence, never measured power.
7. **Null audits are journalable** (refuting "silence is unreadable" in its strong form): the corpus's own entries are realized, witnessed defects, so "this sealed artifact survived every previously-escaped defect kind, replayed" is a calibration-free negative report; audit exposure becomes a queryable primary. Its residue — power against C, never against undiscovered defects — is B6 one step up, stated.
8. **Impeachment semantics**: pure query, seals immutable, `check_dependencies` refuses impeached dependencies at open time; transitive coverage is the consumer's fold over `depends_on`, exposed as a convenience query, never a write (RFC 5280 path-validation shape).
9. **Corpus-finder provenance** is journal-cited metadata, **never** fed into author gates and **never** deriving exclusions. Here two verifiers conflicted: one demanded generator-found entries be excluded from Ds gating that generator's lines; the other proved an added entry is a monotone kill obligation that can only make the generator's measurement harder, and that finder-derived exclusion would itself become the weapon (self-immunization). The author adjudicates for the second: provenance is carried as a seal primary split (participant-found vs independent), and no gate reads it. Recorded here because it is a judgment, not a proof.
10. **Open lines at a demotion crossing**: never closed by cascade. Under a class whose policy bit gates Seal, they close *at their own Seal attempt* with a new published fault carrying the primaries, reproducible by the existing failed-seal re-drive.

## Ancestry the paper must cite

The loop's prior formalization at the argumentation level is **Bloomfield & Rushby's Assurance 2.0 / eliminative argumentation** (defeaters confirmed, refuted, or accepted with recorded rationale — never silently dropped); the ratchet is that discipline mechanized, with the delta stated: established escapes are replayed ground-truth kills, not dispositioned prose, and the non-covering successor is *refused by the kernel*, not flagged for an assessor. Corpus: escape-to-test regression practice and mined-from-real-faults mutation (Tufano 2018; Beller et al. 2021; Defects4J). Demotion: flaky-test quarantine and SRE error budgets (the practices that lack a verified false-negative channel — which is what the escape object creates), and browser root-program CA distrust as the institutional precedent. Impeachment: CRL/OCSP/Sigstore shape, with the two properties the searches found **no prior formalization for**: *non-discretion* (revocation entailed by a replayable proof, not an issuer's act) and *charge totality* (mechanical consequence for every vouching checker). TUF-rollback and conformal-recalibration equivalences were refuted; Gibbs & Candès is cited as the shape a statistical calibration theorem would have and why this layer does not claim one.

## The honest headline

"Calibration" is kept in the repo's metrological, mode-3 sense — the reference corpus is ratcheted against verified real defects — with the statistical sense explicitly filed under *What is not proved*: the theorems are about **the ledger of found misses, never the distribution of real ones**, and the C-section must say so or the theorem list itself performs the laundering the seal's power field was designed to prevent. What is proved is a shorter sentence than the brief's: **forgetting is loud.** Every found miss has a mandatory, attributed, replayable consequence; what was never found, the ledger cannot see, and says so.

Verdict shape: 4 findings refuted outright per lens on average; one full "holds" (the charge unit); everything else holds-weakened into the constraints above. Paper disposition per the round: a section of the RGA paper, earning separate standing only through a kernel held to the rga/core.py standard — which is what gets built next.
